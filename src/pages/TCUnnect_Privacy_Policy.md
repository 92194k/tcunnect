# TCUnnect — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: [insert date before publishing]**

This Privacy Policy explains what information TCUnnect ("we," "us") collects, how we use it, and your rights regarding it, in accordance with the **Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (RA 10173)**.

## 1. What We Collect

| Category | Examples | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Account info | Email, password (encrypted), full name | Login and identification |
| Profile info | Department, year level, program, bio, interests, profile photo | Powers Discover and matching |
| Verification data | School ID/COE photo, live selfie | Confirms you are a real TCU student — required before you can use the App |
| Activity data | Likes, matches, messages, profile views, feed posts/comments, upvotes | Core app functionality |
| Payment data | Payment method chosen (GCash/Maya/QRPh), purchase record | Premium upgrade — **actual payment credentials are handled entirely by PayMongo, never stored by us** |
| Reports & moderation | Reports you file, reports filed against you, screenshots you attach | Safety and moderation |
| Technical data | IP address, device/browser info, login timestamps | Security and abuse prevention |

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To create and secure your account, and verify your identity as a TCU student.
- To power Discover, matching, messaging, and the Campus Feed.
- To process your Premium purchase (via PayMongo).
- To review reports and enforce our Terms of Service, including suspending or banning accounts.
- To send you account-related emails (verification, password reset, notifications).
- To improve and maintain the Service.

We do **not** sell your personal information to third parties, and we do not use your data for advertising.

## 3. Your ID and Selfie Photos

Your ID/COE and selfie are stored in a **private, access-restricted location** that only you and our verification reviewers can access — they are never shown publicly, never shown to other users, and never used for any purpose other than confirming you're a real TCU student.

## 4. What Other Users Can See

| You are... | What's visible to others |
|---|---|
| Any user (free) | Department, year, and shared interests of people who liked/viewed you — identity blurred |
| Premium user | Full identity of people who liked/viewed you |
| Anyone | Your profile photo, department, year, bio, and interests are visible to other verified users in Discover |
| Feed poster | Your posts and comments on the Campus Feed are **never** attributed to your identity — this is structural, not a setting |

Your email, ID/selfie photos, exact date of birth, and payment details are **never** visible to other users under any circumstance.

## 5. Data Sharing with Third Parties

We share data only with service providers necessary to run TCUnnect:

- **Supabase** — hosts our database, authentication, and file storage.
- **PayMongo** — processes Premium payments (GCash/Maya/QRPh). We never see or store your actual payment credentials.
- We do not share your data with advertisers, data brokers, or any party outside of operating the Service, except where required by law or to protect user safety (e.g., responding to a valid legal request, or reporting credible threats of harm).

## 6. Data Retention & Deletion

- Your data is retained for as long as your account is active.
- If you request account deletion, your profile data, matches, messages, likes, blocks, profile views, verification history, and purchase records are **permanently deleted** from our active database.
- A minimal record (containing no personal details) is retained solely to prevent the deleted account's login from being reused, until an administrator completes final account removal.
- We may retain moderation records (e.g., that an account was banned and why) for a reasonable period after deletion, where necessary for safety, audit, or legal compliance.

## 7. Your Rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to:
- Be informed about how your data is processed (this document).
- Access the personal data we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate data (via your Profile settings).
- Object to or withdraw consent for processing, where applicable.
- Request deletion of your data (via Settings, or by contacting us).
- Lodge a complaint with the **National Privacy Commission (NPC)** if you believe your rights have been violated.

To exercise these rights, contact us at **[insert contact email]**.

## 8. Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices, including encrypted storage, access-restricted file buckets, and row-level database security policies, to protect your information. No system is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Children's Privacy

TCUnnect is restricted to users 18 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If we become aware that an underage individual has created an account, it will be removed.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated via the App or email before they take effect.
